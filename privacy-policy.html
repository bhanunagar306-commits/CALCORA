CALCORA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

CALCORA is a calculator application. The current version does not require an account and does not include analytics or an application backend.

Information stored on your device

Calculation history, optional notes, theme selection, sound preference and display precision are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. You can clear calculation history inside the app or clear the site's storage through your browser settings.

Network requests

The app does not intentionally send calculator expressions, results or history to CALCORA servers. When hosted online, your browser necessarily requests the app files from the hosting provider, which may process standard server logs under its own policies.

Children

CALCORA is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app features change. For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer through the repository's public issue or contact channel.
